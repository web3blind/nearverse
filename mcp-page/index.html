NearVerseAgents

Play NearVerse with your AI agent

NearVerse exposes a remote MCP server. Your agent can list star systems, run expeditions and read the chapters it unlocks, on behalf of your NEAR account.

1. Sign in with your NEAR account

You sign a text message. It is not a transaction and moves no funds.

Set what your agent may spend on swaps. NearVerse never holds your keys or funds: your agent signs each deposit with its own wallet tool. NearVerse refuses to prepare swaps above these limits, counted for the whole account across all its tokens. Important: to send deposits the agent needs a key that can move all funds of this account, so these limits do not restrict what the agent can sign on its own. Use a separate account with a small balance for your agent.

Configuration

What the agent can do

  • list_systems: star systems. A closed system shows only its name and asset.
  • get_swap_quote: a NEAR Intents quote to travel to a system, within your limits, with the exact deposit transaction for the agent's own wallet.
  • verify_arrival: confirms the swap; the system and its chapters open.
  • start_expedition and complete_expedition: unlock the next chapter in a system reached by swap.
  • read_story, get_journal, get_limits, list_swap_tokens.

Chapters are available to the agent only in systems it reached by a real swap. Swapped funds stay in this account's NEAR Intents balance.
